We want you to love every MOMEN piece. This Returns & Exchanges Policy explains when a piece can be returned, exchanged or refunded.

Please read it before placing your order. Nothing in this policy affects any rights you have under applicable Nigerian consumer protection law.

1. Ready-to-Wear Pieces

Time Limit

You must contact us within 24 hours of receiving your order to request a return or exchange. Requests made after 24 hours cannot be accepted, except where an item is faulty.

Condition

To be accepted, a ready-to-wear item must be returned:

  • Unworn, unwashed and unaltered.
  • Clean, free from stains, odours, perfume or make-up marks.
  • In its original packaging, with all tags and labels attached.

Items that do not meet these conditions will be sent back to you and will not be exchanged or refunded.

Exchange or Refund

Where your return is accepted, we will offer an exchange for another size or another piece of equal value.

A refund will be given where:

  • The item is faulty or damaged on arrival.
  • We sent the wrong item, size or colour.
  • An exchange is not possible because the replacement is unavailable.

Any other refund is at MOMEN's discretion.

Return Delivery Costs

If the return is due to our error, or the item is faulty, MOMEN covers the cost of return delivery. In all other cases, the cost of returning the item, and of sending an exchanged item back to you, is paid by the customer. Original delivery fees are not refundable unless the return is due to our error.

2. Made-to-Measure and Bespoke Pieces

Made-to-measure and bespoke pieces are produced specifically for you, using your measurements and choices. For this reason, they cannot be returned, exchanged or refunded for a change of mind, including a change of style, colour or fabric preference.

Fit and Workmanship

If your piece has a fault in workmanship, or does not match the measurements and specifications you provided, please contact us within 24 hours of delivery with your order number and photographs. We will review it and, where the issue is confirmed, correct it with an alteration or remake free of charge.

Alterations needed because the measurements provided were inaccurate may be charged. Our measurement guide explains how to take each measurement correctly, and we recommend reviewing your measurements carefully before submitting them.

3. How to Request a Return or Exchange

  • Contact us within 24 hours of delivery, from your order page or by email, with your order number, the item and the reason.
  • We will review your request and confirm whether it is accepted, and explain how and where to send the item.
  • Once we receive and inspect the item, we will process your exchange, alteration or refund.

Please do not send items back without contacting us first.

4. Refunds

Approved refunds are made to the original payment method. Once a refund is approved, it can take up to 10 business days to appear in your account, depending on your bank or card provider.
