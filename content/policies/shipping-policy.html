This Delivery Policy explains how and where MOMEN delivers your order, how long it takes and what happens once your order leaves our atelier.

1. Where We Deliver

We deliver to every state in Nigeria and to most countries internationally.

If we are unable to deliver to your address, we will contact you before dispatching your order and, where we cannot find an alternative, refund your payment in full.

2. Delivery Fees

Delivery fees depend on your location and the delivery option you choose. The exact fee is calculated and shown at checkout before you pay, together with any free-delivery offer that applies to your order.

3. Delivery Times

Ready-to-wear pieces are dispatched once your payment is confirmed. The estimated delivery time for your location is shown at checkout.

Made-to-measure and bespoke pieces are produced after your order and measurements are confirmed. The production time is shown on each product page and at checkout, and delivery time is added once your piece is ready. Where available, express production can be selected at checkout for an additional fee.

All delivery times are estimates. Delays can occasionally occur due to courier operations, public holidays, weather, customs or other circumstances outside our control. We will keep you informed if your order is delayed.

4. Order Tracking

Once your order is dispatched, we will notify you by email. You can follow the progress of your order at any time from your account or from your order confirmation.

5. Receiving Your Order

Please make sure your delivery address and phone number are correct and complete when placing your order, as the courier may need to contact you to complete delivery.

When your order arrives, please check the package. If it appears damaged or tampered with, or an item is missing or incorrect, contact us within 24 hours of delivery with your order number and photographs so we can resolve it promptly.

If a delivery fails because the address or phone number provided was incorrect, or because the recipient was unavailable, additional delivery charges may apply to redeliver the order.

6. International Orders

International delivery times vary by destination and are shown at checkout as an estimate.

International orders may be subject to import duties, taxes and customs charges in the destination country. These charges are not included in our prices or delivery fees and are the responsibility of the recipient. MOMEN has no control over these charges or over delays caused by customs clearance.

7. Pickup

Where pickup is offered for your location, you can choose it at checkout. We will contact you when your order is ready to collect.
