At MOMEN, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, process and protect your personal information when you visit our website, create an account, place an order, contact us or interact with our services.

By using our website, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect different categories of personal information depending on how you interact with MOMEN.

Personal and Contact Information

This may include:

  • Full name.
  • Email address.
  • Phone number.
  • Billing and delivery addresses.
  • Account login information.

Order and Transaction Information

When you make a purchase, we may collect:

  • Products purchased.
  • Order numbers and purchase history.
  • Payment status and transaction references.
  • Delivery preferences.
  • Customer service and order-related communications.

We do not intend to collect or store your complete payment card details. Payment information is handled by the relevant payment service provider.

Measurement and Customisation Information

For made-to-measure and bespoke orders, we may collect personal measurements and garment preferences, including:

  • Chest, waist and hip measurements.
  • Shoulder, sleeve and trouser measurements.
  • Garment length and other relevant body measurements.
  • Preferred garment styles, fabrics and colours.
  • Reference images and notes you choose to upload.
  • Other specifications necessary to fulfil your order.

This information is used exclusively for relevant garment production, fitting and customer service purposes.

Technical Information

When you visit our website, certain technical information may be collected automatically, including:

  • IP address.
  • Browser type and device information.
  • Operating system.
  • Pages visited and browsing activity.
  • Website interaction and performance information.
  • Cookies and similar tracking technologies.

2. How We Use Your Information

MOMEN collects and processes personal information for legitimate business purposes, including:

  • Creating and managing customer accounts.
  • Processing and fulfilling orders.
  • Producing made-to-measure and bespoke garments.
  • Processing payments and confirming transactions.
  • Arranging deliveries.
  • Providing order updates and customer support.
  • Responding to enquiries, complaints and feedback.
  • Improving our website, products and shopping experience.
  • Detecting and preventing fraudulent transactions.
  • Maintaining business and financial records.
  • Meeting applicable legal and regulatory obligations.
  • Sending promotional communications where permitted and, where required, with your consent.

We will not use your personal information for purposes that are incompatible with the purposes for which it was collected, unless permitted by applicable law.

3. Legal Basis for Processing

We process personal information in accordance with the Nigeria Data Protection Act, 2023 and other applicable data protection requirements.

Depending on the circumstances, our legal basis for processing may include:

  • Contractual necessity: To process and fulfil your purchases and provide requested services.
  • Legal obligations: To comply with applicable laws, financial recordkeeping and regulatory requirements.
  • Legitimate interests: To operate, secure and improve our business, prevent fraud and provide customer support, where these interests do not override your rights.
  • Consent: Where you have voluntarily agreed to a specific use of your information, such as certain marketing communications or optional cookies.

Where consent is the applicable legal basis, you may withdraw it at any time.

4. How We Share Your Information

MOMEN does not sell your personal information to third parties.

However, we may share relevant information with trusted service providers where necessary to operate our business and fulfil your orders.

These may include:

Payment Providers

Payment processors and financial institutions that facilitate secure payment transactions.

Delivery and Logistics Partners

Courier companies and delivery providers that require your name, phone number and delivery address to fulfil your order.

Technology and Website Providers

Website hosting providers, e-commerce platforms, email delivery services, analytics providers and other technical service providers that support our website and business operations.

Professional and Regulatory Authorities

Legal advisers, auditors, regulators and government authorities where disclosure is required or permitted by law.

We expect service providers handling personal information on our behalf to maintain appropriate confidentiality and security measures.

5. Cookies and Website Technologies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to improve your browsing and shopping experience.

Cookies may help us:

  • Remember your shopping cart.
  • Maintain your login session.
  • Understand how visitors use our website.
  • Improve website functionality and performance.
  • Remember certain preferences.
  • Support analytics and marketing activities where applicable.

You may manage cookies through your browser settings and, where provided, our website cookie preferences.

Disabling certain cookies may affect some website features.

6. How We Protect Your Information

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, loss or destruction.

These measures may include:

  • Restricted access to customer information.
  • Secure website connections.
  • Appropriate access controls.
  • Secure payment processing through third-party providers.
  • Internal procedures for handling customer information.
  • Periodic reviews of our security practices.

Although we take reasonable steps to protect your information, no online platform or electronic transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. How Long We Retain Your Information

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected.

Retention periods may depend on:

  • The nature of your relationship with MOMEN.
  • The duration of your customer account.
  • Order and transaction records.
  • Legal and regulatory recordkeeping requirements.
  • The resolution of disputes or complaints.
  • Legitimate business and security requirements.

Measurement information may be retained while needed for ongoing customer orders, repeat purchases or alterations. If you have an account, you can view, update or delete your saved measurement profiles at any time from the Measurements page. Where information is no longer required, we will take appropriate steps to delete or securely dispose of it, subject to applicable retention obligations.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request deletion of your personal information.
  • Request restriction of certain processing activities.
  • Object to certain uses of your information.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Request a portable copy of your personal information where applicable.
  • Lodge a complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details below.

We may need to verify your identity before responding to certain requests.

Some information may need to be retained where required by law or necessary to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.
